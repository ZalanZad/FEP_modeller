# FEP Writing Standards: IC papers, investment emails, deck text

Audience: the FEP Investment Committee, partners and co-investors. They are busy, numerate and sceptical.
They want the decision, the reasons and the risks, in that order.

## Voice
- Professional, concise, factual. Write as an investment professional, not as a chat message.
- Lead with the conclusion (the answer first, then the support). One idea per paragraph.
- Active voice, short sentences, no filler ("we would like to", "it is important to note").
- No hype or adjectives such as "excellent", "huge" or "very". Let the numbers carry the weight.
- Be balanced: every recommendation names its main risk and the mitigant.
- Never round a number differently from the deck. Every figure ties to the model (`Sheet!Cell`).

## Numbers
- € m to 1 dp, with negatives in brackets: €(3.5)m. Percent to 1 dp. Multiples 1.25x. Points to 1 dp where fractional.
- State the basis: "post-tax, FEP share, EU turbine case, RtB exit on 31-Dec-2029".
- Use "c." for approximations only when the model value is not shown.

## Investment email (EN default; HU on request)
Structure:
1. Subject: `<Project> – <decision requested> – <date>`
2. Opening line: what we ask for, and by when.
3. Recommendation: 2–3 sentences.
4. Key rationale: 3–5 bullets, each = point + number.
5. Economics: a small table or 3 bullets (RtB price PV, FEP proceeds, HTM IRR).
6. Key risks and mitigants: 2–4 bullets.
7. Next steps / timeline, then the attachments.
Length: it must fit on one screen (≈250–350 words).

## Hungarian register
Use formal business Hungarian (magázó / impersonal), as in an investment memo: "Javasoljuk…", "A Befektetési Bizottság jóváhagyását kérjük…".
Avoid anglicisms where a standard Hungarian term exists, but keep the technical tokens (RtB, COD, IRR, MOIC, NPV, BESS, PPA, MVA, ÖSZ codes) unchanged.

## Deck text
- Action titles state the conclusion ("Municipal contribution buys 20 points at <0.2 pp HTM IRR cost").
- Subtitle: the so-what in one sentence. Bullets ≤ 2 lines. Footnotes give the assumptions and the source.
